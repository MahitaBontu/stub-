Where do these numbers come from?

Stub estimates savings using your selected 9.5% annual financing rate.

Cost avoided per invoice: Invoice amount × rate × days saved ÷ 365.

Estimated annual savings: Cost avoided per invoice × 12 similar invoices.

These estimates depend on your financing costs and actual payment timing.
