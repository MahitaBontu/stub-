Where do these numbers come from?

Stub estimates savings using an assumed 9.5% annual financing rate.

Cost avoided per invoice:
Invoice amount × 9.5% × Days saved ÷ 365

Estimated annual savings:
Cost avoided per invoice × 12 invoices

Estimates assume 12 similar invoices per year. Actual savings depend on financing costs and payment timing.
